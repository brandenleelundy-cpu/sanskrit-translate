Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What this site is

A free English, Hindi and Sanskrit translator. It has no accounts and asks for no personal details.

Text you translate

When you press Translate, your text is sent to our translation server, hosted on Hugging Face Spaces, which runs the IndicTrans2 model and sends the translation back. Our code does not save your text. The hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Please don't enter passwords, ID numbers or other sensitive information.

Hosting

The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
